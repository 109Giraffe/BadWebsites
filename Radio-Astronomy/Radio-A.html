What is radio astronomy?

Radio astronomy is a brach of astronomy where radio telescopes are used to study radio waves emitted by celestial objects. It alows us to observe phenomena that are invisible to the human eye and optical telescopes. By analyzing these radio signals we can see factors like the compositions and the structures of objects in space.

How does it work

Why Did I get into Radio Astronomy?

When I was younger, I was planespotting at my local airport when someone showed me a radio scanner where you could hear the ATC speaking and the pilots speaking too. I immediatley told my dad to order me the same one and I used it for a while. Then my dad got an even bigger and better Radio scanner that we brought everywhere. After a while we started buying new antennas and we got a SDR(Software defined radio) dongle which I still use, this will be important later. Then someone in my dads work, who is very into radios, gave my dad a very expensive HAM radio to play about with which influenced my dad to get his Amateur radio license. Over time we have gotten a wide veriety of radios (we probably have 10 in total). Then one day I disovered what radio astronomy was and it re-lit something inside me from when I was younger when I was extremely interested in space so I decided to look into it further and hence, we ended here.
